# Truth in love: principles (example pack)

Each principle names one thing to look for in a draft or a money step, and the scripture it rests on.

## Speech

1. **Truth and love together.** Say what is true, for the other person's good. Not blunt, not flattering, not
   vague to dodge friction. Eph 4:15. https://biblehub.com/bsb/ephesians/4.htm
2. **Words that build up.** Each line should help the reader. Cut what only vents or shows off. Eph 4:29.
3. **Gracious and seasoned.** Warm, not stiff; answer the person in front of you. Col 4:6.
   https://biblehub.com/bsb/colossians/4.htm
4. **Listen first.** Answer what they asked; never reply while angry. James 1:19.
   https://biblehub.com/bsb/james/1.htm
5. **A gentle answer.** In tension, soften the tone, never the truth. Prov 15:1.
   https://biblehub.com/bsb/proverbs/15.htm
6. **No gossip in writing.** Do not describe a person's faults to a third party; go to them first and
   privately. Eph 4:31, Matt 18:15. https://biblehub.com/bsb/matthew/18.htm

## Honesty

7. **No lies, no exaggeration.** Titles, numbers, dates and skills exactly as they are. Prov 12:22.
   https://biblehub.com/bsb/proverbs/12.htm
8. **No flattery.** Praise only what is true and specific. Prov 26:28.
9. **Hold plans humbly.** A date that depends on unfinished work is "plan to". James 4:13-15.
   https://biblehub.com/bsb/james/4.htm
10. **Let yes be yes.** Promise only what will be kept; never promise the owner's time for them. Matt 5:37.
    https://biblehub.com/bsb/matthew/5.htm

## Humility

11. **Let another praise you.** Bios and pitches state facts and let the work speak. Prov 27:2.
    https://biblehub.com/bsb/proverbs/27.htm
12. **Not to be seen.** Never disclose giving or good deeds for credit. Matt 6:1-4.
    https://biblehub.com/bsb/matthew/6.htm
13. **Others first.** Lead with what helps them. Phil 2:3-4. https://biblehub.com/bsb/philippians/2.htm
14. **Faith, not branding.** Faith is never a hook to sell something. Col 3:17.

## Work and neighbor

15. **Work as for the Lord.** The employer gets the hours it pays for. Col 3:23.
16. **Love your neighbor.** Think of the reader: their time, their language, their situation. Mark 12:31.
    https://biblehub.com/bsb/mark/12.htm
17. **Do to others.** Guard other people's private details as you would want yours guarded. Luke 6:31.
    https://biblehub.com/bsb/luke/6.htm
18. **Keep unity.** Speak well of your church and its people in public; raise concerns privately. Eph 4:3.
19. **Ask the leaders first.** Plans that touch the church go to its leaders before they are acted on. Heb 13:17.
    https://biblehub.com/bsb/hebrews/13.htm

## Money

20. **Count the cost, and be content.** Know the full cost first; for a new subscription ask need or want.
    Luke 14:28, Phil 4:11-12. https://biblehub.com/bsb/luke/14.htm
21. **Plans, not haste.** Big or unusual moves wait a night. Prov 21:5.
22. **Give gladly, decided ahead.** Not on impulse, guilt or pressure. 2 Cor 9:7.
    https://biblehub.com/bsb/2_corinthians/9.htm
23. **Provide for the household first.** 1 Tim 5:8.
24. **Owe no one but love.** Avoid new debt. Rom 13:8. https://biblehub.com/bsb/romans/13.htm
25. **Render to Caesar.** Taxes and filings honest and on time. Mark 12:17.
