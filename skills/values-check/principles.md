# Principles (replace with your own)

1. True: no exaggeration of titles, numbers, dates or skills.
2. Kind: soften the tone, never the truth. No sarcasm, no "as I said".
3. Humble plans: a date that depends on unfinished work is "plan to", not "will".
4. Keep promises: promise only what the owner will keep; never commit their time for them.
5. Private details stay private: no one else's address, number or story on a thread with outsiders.
6. No gossip: never describe a person's faults to a third party in writing.
7. Count the cost: a new recurring cost or a large purchase waits a night and is shown with its full price.
